Our Story

About Pravah

Pravah means "flow" — the flow of ideas, art, and energy that moves through every performer on our stage.

Pravah is our college's annual cultural fest — a single-day celebration of theatre, dance, music, art, and words. Every year on 10th July, students from colleges across the city come together to compete, collaborate, and create memories that outlast the applause.

What started as a small skit night has grown into a full-day festival with six competitions running on one main stage — but the spirit remains the same: give every performer a real audience and a fair shot at the spotlight.

Event Schedule

One day, one stage, six competitions. Here's how the day unfolds.

9:00 AM
Reporting & Registration Desk Opens

Teams check in, collect participant badges, and confirm slot order.

10:00 AM
Opening Ceremony

Welcome address, lamp lighting, and introduction of judges.

10:45 AM
Skit Competition

Main stage. Teams perform in the order assigned at check-in.

12:30 PM
Fine Arts (On-spot)

Theme announced; 90-minute painting/sketching window in the art hall.

1:30 PM
Lunch Break

Food stalls open on the lawn for all participants and visitors.

2:30 PM
Debate Round

Surprise topic revealed; pairs argue for and against, 6 minutes each.

3:30 PM
Group Dance

Main stage. Full lighting and sound setup for choreography rounds.

5:00 PM
Singing Battle

Solo and group vocal performances, backed by the in-house band.

6:30 PM
Stand-up Comedy

Open-mic style solo sets to close out the competitive rounds.

7:30 PM
Prize Distribution & Closing

Winners announced across all six competitions, followed by a closing performance.
